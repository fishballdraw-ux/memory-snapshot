Memory Snapshot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · App ID: com.fishb.memorysnapshot

English

This policy explains how the mobile game Memory Snapshot (“the Game”), published by FishBall&Hotpot, handles information.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following only on your own device, inside the app’s local storage:

  • Player names that you type in (you may use a nickname).
  • Game progress: unlocked levels, scores and number of games played.
  • Settings: language and music volume.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. The “Ranking” screen only compares players saved on the same device.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Game is free and shows banner and full-screen ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect information from your device, such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and processed by Unity under its own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

When you first open the Game, you are asked whether you allow personalized ads. If you choose “No”, only non-personalized (generic) ads are requested. You can change this choice at any time in SETTING → PERSONALIZED ADS. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and use the privacy icon shown during Unity ads to opt out or request access to or deletion of your data.

Information we do not collect

We (the developer) do not run our own servers and do not receive, sell or share your personal information. The Game has no account system, no in-app purchases and no chat, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. The Game itself works offline; an internet connection is only used to load ads.

Deleting your data

You can delete all saved data at any time by uninstalling the Game or by clearing its storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Memory Snapshot → Storage → Clear data).

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. Ads are requested with an all-ages content setting. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party store

If you download the Game from TapTap, TapTap’s own privacy policy applies to your use of the TapTap platform.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: fishballdraw@gmail.com

繁體中文

本政策說明由 FishBall&Hotpot 發行的手機遊戲《記憶定格 Memory Snapshot》（以下稱「本遊戲」）如何處理資料。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

本遊戲只會在你自己的裝置內（App 的本機儲存空間）保存以下資料：

  • 你輸入的玩家名稱（可以使用暱稱）。
  • 遊戲進度：已解鎖的關卡、分數與遊玩次數。
  • 設定：語言與背景音樂音量。

這些資料不會傳送給我們或任何第三方。「排行榜」只比較同一台裝置上的玩家。

廣告（Unity Ads）

本遊戲為免費遊戲，會顯示由 Unity Ads（Unity Technologies）提供的橫幅廣告及全螢幕廣告。為了顯示及衡量廣告成效，Unity Ads 可能會從你的裝置蒐集資料，例如裝置的廣告 ID、IP 位址、裝置型號與作業系統，以及你與廣告的互動情形。這些資料由 Unity 依其隱私權政策蒐集與處理：Unity 遊戲玩家及應用程式使用者隱私權政策。

第一次開啟本遊戲時，會詢問你是否允許個人化廣告。若你選擇「不允許」，則只會請求非個人化（一般）廣告。你可以隨時在「設定 → 個人化廣告」中更改。你也可以在 Android 裝置設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID，並可透過 Unity 廣告中顯示的隱私圖示選擇退出，或要求存取、刪除你的資料。

我們不蒐集的資料

我們（開發者）沒有自己的伺服器，不會接收、出售或分享你的個人資料。本遊戲沒有帳號系統、沒有內購、沒有聊天功能，也不會存取你的通訊錄、位置、相機、麥克風或相簿。遊戲本身可離線遊玩，網路連線只用於載入廣告。

刪除資料

你可以隨時解除安裝本遊戲，或在裝置設定中清除本遊戲的儲存資料（設定 → 應用程式 → Memory Snapshot → 儲存空間 → 清除資料），即可刪除所有已儲存的資料。

兒童

本遊戲以一般大眾為對象，並非專為兒童設計。廣告以適合所有年齡的內容設定請求。我們不會在知情的情況下蒐集兒童的個人資料。

第三方商店

若你透過 TapTap 下載本遊戲，你使用 TapTap 平台時適用 TapTap 自己的隱私權政策。

政策變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有變更，會在此頁面公布並更新日期。如有疑問，請聯絡：fishballdraw@gmail.com
